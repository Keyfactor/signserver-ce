Timestamp Signers

Timestamps are generally used together with eSignatures to provide proof that a document existed before a certain time, and can also be used together with advanced signatures to ensure long-term validation of archives.

The following signers provide time-stamping functionalities:

  • Timestamp Signer
  • Extended Time Stamp Signer
  • MS Authenticode Timestamp Signer

For more information on timestamp sources, see Time Sources in SignServer.

SignServer as TSA Component

SignServer can be operated as a Time Stamp Authority (TSA) unit, issuing digitally signed timestamps compliant with:

  • RFC 3161

  • Microsoft Authenticode

  • ETSI/eIDAS for Qualified Electronic Time-Stamps (EU Regulation No 910/2014)

As SignServer is not a TSA by default, your organization must implement separate policies and procedures.
